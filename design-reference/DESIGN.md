## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric intersection between a vintage listening salon, an avant-garde publication, and a high-concept digital archive. The experience targets discerning art directors, creative collaborators, collectors, and design connoisseurs seeking expressive narrative over sterile utilitarianism. 

The emotional signature is tactile, mysterious, deliberate, and deeply analog. The visual language blends:
- **Tactile Skeuomorphism & Archival Textures:** Micro-interactions mimic physical sleeves, vinyl RPM dials, catalog index tabs, wax stamps, and archival card mounts.
- **Editorial High-Fashion Layouts:** Typographic scale contrasts pair cinematic serifs with dense archival mono notations, utilizing deliberate asymmetric margins and intentional negative space.
- **Experimental Interaction:** Fluid motion echoes turntable physics, heavy paper drags, and light-leaked optical framing, grounding forward-thinking digital craft within tangible physical artifacts.

## Colors

The palette derives from physical catalog relics: oxidized velvet, wax seals, sun-bleached newsprint, and vinyl lacquer. While configured primarily in dark mode to mirror an atmospheric vault, it fluidly incorporates warm ivory surfaces as dimensional layers.

- **Primary Royal Crimson (`#811515`):** The signature thematic pigment. Evokes vintage velvet listening booths, sealing wax, and darkroom safelights. Used for high-impact accents, active playback states, hero moments, and focal interaction targets.
- **Deep Charcoal (`#191715`):** The foundational base canvas. A rich, warm pitch black carrying the organic depth of pressed vinyl and unexposed film emulsion rather than synthetic digital black.
- **Warm Vintage Ivory (`#F2EBDD`):** The primary light ink and surface layer. Used for primary typography, editorial placards, and paper sleeve surfaces.
- **Dusty Rose (`#C98D8D`):** A soft, faded tonal midpoint representing aged dye and worn linen bindings. Ideal for secondary text, metadata timestamps, and subtle divider rules.
- **Accent Golden Wax (`#D4A373`):** An opulent, warm metallic patina. Used for special edition markers, audio scrubbers, precision track labels, and tertiary interactive badges.
- **Muted Beige (`#D8CBB8`):** The supporting parchment tint. Applied to surface containers, table borders, and structural framing.

## Typography

Typography establishes an archival tension between three typographic paradigms:

1. **Playfair Display (Editorial Display Serifs):** Captures the tactile pedigree of bespoke art monographs and 20th-century sleeve jackets. Used with tight letter-spacing for expansive headlines, editorial pull-quotes, and chapter headers. Italic variations are reserved for conceptual accents and artist attributions.
2. **Space Grotesk (Archival Sans-Serif):** Provides modern geometric legibility with idiosyncratic ink traps that mimic mechanical press bleed. Assigned to body narrative, navigation nodes, project outlines, and interactive button text.
3. **Space Mono (Catalog & Metadata Monospace):** Emulates typewritten accession numbers, vinyl matrix run-out etchings, recording timecodes, and technical specifications. Used exclusively in uppercase for labels, tags, counters, and structural indices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a 12-column asymmetric fluid grid rooted in modernist Swiss editorial design and physical scrapbook collages:

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, 56px (`margin: 3.5rem`) outer frame margins, 24px (`gutter: 1.5rem`) column gutters. Supports offset editorial pacing, such as a 7-column artwork canvas paired with a 5-column catalog index panel.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 32px (`margin-tablet: 2rem`) margins, 20px (`gutter-tablet: 1.25rem`) gutters. Content shifts to paired dual-column configurations and docked archival drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px (`margin-mobile: 1.25rem`) margins, 14px (`gutter-mobile: 0.875rem`) gutters. Layout condenses into single-column sequential stacks reminiscent of a physical contact sheet or vertical record bin.

Spacing rhythm prioritizes breathing room for artifacts, letting imagery push past grid boundaries while strictly snapping typography to an 8px base rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through tactile layering, material translucency, and physical object staging rather than generic digital drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (The Archive Floor):** Deep charcoal canvas (`#191715`) with a calibrated CSS noise overlay (analog grain filter at 4% opacity) acting as the base plane.
- **Level 1 (Cardstock & Inlay Sleeves):** Muted surfaces (`#191715` tinted with 6% `#F2EBDD` or `#811515`) framed by subtle 1px low-contrast borders (`#D8CBB8` at 15% opacity).
- **Level 2 (Active Artifacts & Overlays):** Archival panels and sleeve modals elevated with ambient, diffused shadows: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.2)`.
- **Level 3 (Wax Seals, Pinned Markers & Drag Elements):** Focused elements cast hard, directional contact shadows (`2px 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`) to replicate physical scrapbook ephemera sitting proud of the page.

## Shapes

The design system enforces an uncompromising sharp architectural geometry (`roundedness: 0`). 

Everything is clipped with razor-sharp edges—reminiscent of guillotine paper cutters, vinyl LP slipcases, unmounted film strips, and museum archival boxes. The sole circular geometry permitted across the system is strictly reserved for the vinyl record disc itself, needle arm pivots, and circular pressed wax stamps.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary ("Wax Seal / Stamped"):* Razor-sharp rectangular blocks filled with Royal Crimson (`#811515`), featuring Warm Vintage Ivory (`#F2EBDD`) typography in `Space Mono` uppercase. Hover triggers a subtle micro-shift mimicking a physical stamp press accompanied by an Accent Golden Wax border (`#D4A373`).
  - *Secondary ("Archival Index"):* Transparent fill, 1px solid `#D8CBB8` (30% opacity) border with Dusty Rose (`#C98D8D`) text. Hover state inverts background to `#F2EBDD` and text to `#191715`.
- **Chips & Tags:**
  - Compact monospaced badges (`label-sm`) resembling catalog spine labels. Styled with a 1px border of `#D4A373` and `#191715` background. Prefixed with archival symbols or catalog numbers (e.g., `CAT. 129-A`).
- **Cards (The "Vinyl Sleeve"):**
  - Rigid rectangular containers with 1px border in `#D8CBB8` at 15% opacity. On hover, the inner visual media lifts slightly while an inner dark vinyl edge slides outward 24px to the right, revealing track metadata and archival notes.
- **Lists & Catalog Indices:**
  - Border-separated ledger rows (`border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(216, 203, 184, 0.15)`). Structured with monospaced track indices on the left, italicized Playfair Display project titles in the center, and timecodes or format badges aligned right. Hovering highlights the row in `#811515` at 12% opacity.
- **Inputs & Search:**
  - Minimal underline-only inputs (`border-bottom: 1px solid #D8CBB8`), with `#F2EBDD` input text and placeholders styled in muted `#C98D8D`. Active focus states illuminate the underline to Accent Golden Wax (`#D4A373`) with an adjacent blinking monospaced cursor.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Square, non-rounded toggles. Checked states display an analog "X" or filled inner square in Crimson (`#811515`) reminiscent of vintage gallery audit checklists.
- **Audio Scrubber & Needle Control (Custom Component):**
  - A horizontal groove-line styled like an LP track incision. The playhead needle is represented by a 1px vertical hairline with a small Golden Wax circular indicator that drags with turntable damping resistance.
